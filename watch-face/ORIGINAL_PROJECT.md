# Original YAMAKAGE project

Original project: YAMAKAGE
Original developer: Ichimura Tomoo
License: MIT (see `LICENSE`)

This watch-face adaptation is intended to preserve the original YAMAKAGE focus:

- terrain-aware sunlight and moonlight
- an outdoor/mountain visual identity
- useful information rather than decorative weather-face clutter
- the full app remains the richer interactive experience (GPS acquisition, Panorama, SkyPlot, Radar, compass-relative views)

The watch face is designed as a standalone glanceable extension, not a replacement for the original application.

## Original icon resources reused in alpha 6

The sunrise/sunset glyphs included in `resources/fonts/YamakageWatchAppIcons_40.*` and their character mapping are copied from the original YAMAKAGE watch app by Ichimura Tomoo. They are intentionally reused to keep this watch-face adaptation visually faithful to the original project and remain covered by the included MIT license and attribution.

## Watch-face alpha 7 note

The alpha 7 watch-face adaptation continues to preserve the original Device App as the authoritative full YAMAKAGE experience. App detection/launch integration uses the original manifest ID. Any future complication publisher changes to the Device App should remain additive and should not remove or simplify the original Panorama, SkyPlot, Radar, GPS, or terrain-analysis workflows.
